Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

In short: NOMAD: Silk Road Empire collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't need the internet to play.

What the game stores

The game saves your cities and a few settings on your own device. These are your towns, campaign progress and medals, chosen language, and music and sound levels. They never leave your device and are not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them. If you use iCloud or device backups, Apple may include them in your backup under Apple's own privacy policy.

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics or tracking services, and it does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not sell or share any data, because it doesn't have any.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports. They do not identify you. We use them only to fix bugs. You can switch this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

If you email us

If you write to us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to answer you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed. We never add you to a mailing list.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ads or third-party fonts. Like any web host, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for a short time to keep the site secure and running.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law (the UK GDPR and the GDPR) you have the right to ask what data we hold about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Apart from support emails, we hold none. Email us about any privacy question.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example because the game gains a new feature that needs data, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
