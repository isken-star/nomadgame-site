Common questions

Answers from the council

Nobody moves into my homes

Every home needs a road along one of its sides, and that road must join the Silk Road, where settlers arrive. A second row of homes can be reached through the row in front, but a third row gets nobody. The build preview shows amber tiles where no road reaches.

My homes rise a level, then drop back

Each level needs its services to keep coming: water, food from a market, a shrine's visits and more. Tap a home to see what it needs to rise and what it is short of. The overlays button shows at a glance which streets lack water, food or faith.

A building says it has no workers

Nine in twenty of your people can work. When jobs outnumber workers, the last buildings in line stay empty. Build more homes, or open the Work advisor and close a less important building or give one priority.

My fountain or bathhouse is dry

Fountains and bathhouses need piped water. Build a reservoir touching a lake or river, then chain more reservoirs inland. The Pipes overlay shows every tile the pipes reach.

The Great Khan is angry with me

Open the Khan advisor to see what he asks for and how long you have. Your stores hold his share back for you; send it before the time runs out. A gift from your own purse also softens him.

Where is my city saved? Can I move it?

Your cities save themselves on your device every 30 seconds and whenever you leave the game. They move to a new phone with an iCloud or computer backup of your device. There are no online accounts.

How do I change the language or sound?

Tap the gold gear on the main menu. The game speaks English, Русский, Türkçe, Deutsch, Français and Español, and starts in your phone's language if it can.

How do I start over?

In a city, open the menu at the top right and choose New city (in The Endless Steppe) or Restart (in a scenario).
