Produce. Trade. Grow.

A city builder in the spirit of the great classics, set on the Central Asian steppe. Yurts become mudbrick homes, a market becomes a bazaar, and camel caravans carry your town's goods to Samarkand and Bukhara.

Produce

Herds, fields and workshops

Graze sheep, sow wheat, quarry stone and mine iron. Turn wool into felt, clay into pottery and cocoons into silk.

Trade

Caravans of the Silk Road

Five cities each want different goods. Sell at the gate, or send your own camels to a far city for a better price.

Grow

Homes that rise

Every home grows when its needs are met: water, food, faith, tales, schooling and pleasant streets. From a small tent to a wind-tower mansion.

A living town in your hand

Water carriers, shamans, storytellers and tax collectors walk your streets, and each house they pass is served. Lay a road with one drag, plan a whole quarter, then tap Build when you're sure.

Made for the phone: sessions of two minutes or two hours, controls built for a thumb, and a quiet screen that leaves the town in view.

◆

An empire to keep you busy

Three gods of the steppe

Tengri, Umay and Erlik each want shrines and festivals. Pleased gods send rain and children; neglected ones send lightning and plague.

The Great Khan's demands

He asks for horses, felt or gold for his wars. Send them and rise in his favour; refuse three times and his army rides.

Twenty scenarios

The Khan's Road takes you from a first camp to the Golden Horde, across five lands, with medals for the swift.

The Endless Steppe

A sandbox on any land, with a chain of goals that never runs out.

Seasons and hardship

Harsh winters, droughts, floods, earthquakes, sick herds, bandit camps and thieves' dens. Plan ahead or pay for it.

Painted by hand

Felt yurts, adobe and baked brick, turquoise domes, and steppe music for dombra, kobyz and flute.
